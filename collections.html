12 / Ancient steppe tattoo art

The Scythian-Pazyryk Tattoo Art Collection

Celestial Anatomy, inspired by the old mummified tattoos of the Scythian-Pazyryk culture. Preserved in the permafrost of the Altai Mountains near the Ukok Plateau, the body art of nomadic Kurgan warriors and the legendary Lady of Ukok was never mere decoration—it was metaphysical armor. Designed to protect and empower the wearer in life, in battle, and in the afterlife, these sacred motifs fuse the raw instincts of terrestrial beasts with the supernatural vision of celestial birds of prey. Every piece in this collection honors the unmistakable graphic language of 5th-century BC steppe artistry.
